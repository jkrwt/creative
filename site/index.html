Tap a group to open it. Tap a people for its language, dates, sources, and what it became.

Dots are dated events and bars are periods: tap either to go there. With the slider in focus, the arrow keys step ten years, with Shift a hundred and with Alt one; Page Up and Page Down step a period; [ and ] step an event.

How to read the map

  • Soft wash: the language most people spoke
  • Bright field: a named people, or a group of them
  • Stripes: two languages on the same ground
  • Stippled: placement is loose
  • Hatched: outside the survey

Colour and shape show language

Only the branches on the map in this view and year are listed. Select one to highlight it. Each branch has a shape as well as a colour, so nothing depends on colour alone. A shape or a colour may be used again on another continent.
