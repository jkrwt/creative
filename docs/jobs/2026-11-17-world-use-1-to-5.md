# Job: the first five experience items

Dated 17 November 2026. A sub job of the assessment in `docs/reports/experience.md`, at the owner's instruction: "build the first five experience items as one new sub job". This file is the scope. Work outside it goes in Findings.

## What is built

1. **Pick by field, and name on hover.** A tap asks which lettered name, and then which entry, owns that point of the map. A tap that misses looks within 15 pixels. A mouse gets the name of what it is over and a pointing cursor. Touch gets no hover.
2. **Search.** A box above the map finds a people, state, language or group by name, without accents or case. Choosing a result sets the year to the middle of its life, moves to the closest view that holds it, opens its group and selects it.
3. **A better slider.** The periods under the track are buttons. Dated events sit above the track as dots, from the new table `data/events.csv`: tap one to go there, and letting go of the slider within three pixels of one lands on it. The event's name shows beside the year. Keys with the slider in focus: arrows step ten years, with Shift a hundred and with Alt one; Page Up and Page Down step a period; `[` and `]` step an event. A selected entry's life is drawn as a bar under the track, and its card offers "First appears" and "Last appears".
4. **Succession in the card.** Every link in `data/links.csv` that touches the selected entry is a button, worded from that entry's end ("Continues as", "Came from", "Merged into", "Took in"), and goes to the other entry at the year of the link.
5. **The address follows the view.** `#y=1350&v=westafrica&g=western-sudan&s=mali-empire`. It is written a third of a second after the view settles and read on load and when it changes.

## Rules held

The look is unchanged: no new colour, no new motion. Nothing depends on colour alone. Every new control is a real button or input with a label, a white focus ring, and 44 pixels of height on touch. The page still makes no outside request beyond its fonts.

## Gates

1. Tests pass and the validator reports no errors.
2. `tools/ux_check.js` runs every feature in a browser with no page error.
3. Desktop and phone screenshots with the new controls have been looked at.

## Findings

- **Two older bugs were found and fixed on the way.**
  - In some opened groups the mainland vanished: no coast, no fields, only labels. West Africa was one. The coastline ring for Africa and Eurasia crosses the date line at Chukotka, and the viewer placed the whole ring by its first point, which could be a full turn away. Rings are now unwrapped once and placed by their true west and east ends.
  - A band of northern Siberia, about 65 to 69 north and the width of Russia east of the Urals, lay outside every region: hatched, with no wash. `tools/coast.py` clipped Russia's outline with its date-line jump still in it, and the jump cut the band out. The cutter now unwraps a ring before clipping. The dark-land check had not seen it because it samples only inside the region outlines. `site/data/coast.json` is recut.
- **Verified.** 31 tests pass and the validator is clean. `tools/ux_check.js` ran all fifteen steps with no page error: hover on land and sea, tap, search with and without a match, three succession hops, slider keys, an event mark, a period button, and arrival by address with a group opened and an entry selected. A second script with motion on glided from the world to East Asia and opened a group with no error. Looked at: the search card on West Africa, arrival by address, the phone page with the search list open, and eight views after the coastline fix.
- **Not verified.** Whether the hosted artifact page lets the script read and write its address; it works for the page opened as a file. Screen readers. Real touch devices: the phone test was an emulated one. Frame rate: headless Chromium with no GPU gave 18 frames a second on East Asia with motion on, against 25 before this job on one earlier run; the colouring step measured the same as before, so I take the difference for noise, but it is not proven.
- **Seed data added.** 101 dated events across six views, typed from memory and marked `seed`. They need the same check as everything else.
- **Known limits.** Search matches names only, not the older names recorded in notes. A selected entry stays selected when the year leaves its life, with its card still open. On a phone the event dots are close together where history is dense, and neighbours can be hard to tell apart by touch.
